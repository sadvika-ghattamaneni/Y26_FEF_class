About kl university

kl university is a private university located in vaddeswaram, guntur district, andhra pradesh, india. it was established in 1980 by the kl deeksha foundation. the university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in various fields such as engineering, management, law, pharmacy, and sciences.
